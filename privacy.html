Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Hide Images & Videos does not collect, store, process, share, or transmit any personal information or user data.

What the extension does

This extension allows users to hide image and video elements on webpages for a cleaner browsing experience. The modification happens locally inside the user's browser.

Data Collection

The extension does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Browsing history
  • Cookies
  • User activity
  • Website content
  • Credentials or authentication data
  • Location information

Storage

If enabled, local browser storage may be used only to save user preferences (for example: whether hiding images/videos is turned on). These settings remain on the user's device and are never transmitted externally.

Third-party services

The extension does not use analytics, advertising services, or third-party tracking tools.
